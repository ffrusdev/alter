Fonts used by the Alter window (loaded from this folder; nothing is installed on your PC).

Rubik (Bold, ExtraBold)      headings, buttons, letter tiles
Inter (Regular, Medium, SemiBold)   text
JetBrains Mono (Regular)     paths and arguments

All three are open-source fonts from Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com),
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
If this folder is missing, Alter uses Segoe UI and Consolas instead.
